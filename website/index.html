Privacy

Your voice stays
on your Mac.

Psst Free processes everything locally using WhisperKit, Apple's on-device speech recognition framework. There are no servers, no accounts, and no telemetry.

  • No audio uploaded, ever
  • No cloud APIs or third-party services
  • No accounts or sign-ups required
  • All settings stored locally in UserDefaults
