The short version

Psst Free processes your speech entirely on your device. No audio leaves your Mac. We don't collect analytics, don't track you, and don't run any telemetry. Your data stays yours.

What the app does

Psst Free is a macOS menu bar application that converts your speech to text. You press a hotkey, speak, and the transcribed text is pasted into whatever app you're using. That's it.

How audio processing works

This is the most important part: all audio processing happens entirely on your device. Psst Free uses WhisperKit, an on-device speech recognition engine. Your audio is captured by your Mac's microphone, transcribed locally using a machine learning model running on your hardware, and never transmitted anywhere.

No audio data is sent to any server — not ours, not Apple's, not anyone's.

System permissions

Psst Free requests the following macOS permissions, each for a specific functional reason:

  • Microphone — to capture your voice for transcription. Audio is processed locally and discarded after transcription.
  • Accessibility — to register global hotkeys that work regardless of which app is in the foreground.
  • Input Monitoring — to capture hotkey events (key presses) system-wide.
  • Clipboard access — to paste the transcribed text into the active application via the system clipboard.

None of these permissions are used to monitor, log, or transmit your activity.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and preferences (selected transcription model, hotkey configuration, writing style, custom vocabulary, snippets) are stored in macOS UserDefaults — a local storage mechanism on your Mac. This data is never synced to any server.

Psst Free does not maintain any logs, databases, or files containing your transcribed text. Once text is pasted, it exists only in the target application and your clipboard.

Data we collect

None. As of this writing, Psst Free has:

  • No analytics
  • No telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No tracking of any kind
  • No user accounts
  • No server-side components

The app makes network requests only to download the WhisperKit speech recognition model on first use (or when you switch models). These downloads come from Apple's ML model hosting and do not include any identifying information about you.
